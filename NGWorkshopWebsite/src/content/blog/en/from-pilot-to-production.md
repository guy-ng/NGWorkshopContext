The gap between an impressive demo and a system that works every day is not measured only by model accuracy. It is measured by the organization's ability to understand behavior, handle exceptions, and keep operating when a provider or model changes.

## Five readiness questions

1. What is the business outcome, and who owns it?
2. What information enters the system, and who may access it?
3. Which conditions stop the automation and hand work to a person?
4. How do we measure quality, cost, and end-to-end time?
5. Can we explain and reproduce an action after the fact?

If one question has no clear answer, that does not mean the project must stop. It means you have found the right next experiment before scaling it.
