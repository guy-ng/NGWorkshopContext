The NG Workshop Acceleration Package provides a professional and proven foundation for successful Camunda implementation in your organization. It is designed for organizations looking to start working with Camunda quickly, systematically, and with focus, while being guided by experienced experts.

## Package Phases

### Phase 1: Process Discovery Workshop
**Duration: 5 hours on-site + offline analysis**

- Mapping of business processes with stakeholders
- Current state analysis (As-Is)
- Preparation for future state planning (To-Be)
- Process prioritization and selection criteria

### Phase 2: Process Selection & Architecture Design

- Selection of 3 processes for implementation
- To-Be process design
- Solution architecture definition
- Deployment model (SaaS or Self-managed)
- Environment setup (Dev/Test/Prod)
- Integration planning
- Security and DevOps considerations

*Requires participation of infrastructure team representative for coordination and alignment*

### Phase 3: Process Implementation

- Full development of 3 selected processes
- Collaboration with your development team
- Integration, permissions, and configuration
- QA testing with organizational team support
