SCR-09: Time Sync — BQ Technician App

390×844, Material Design 3. Compact utility screen.

Purpose
Synchronize device clock with phone, configure timezone and DST, monitor drift.

Layout

Clock Comparison Card (top, prominent)
Two side-by-side digital clocks: "Phone Time" (left) and "Device Time" (right)
Large time display (HH:MM:SS) in each
Drift badge between them: "+3.2s ahead" or "-1.5s behind"
Drift color: green (<1s), amber (1–10s), red (>10s)

Sync Action
Large primary button: "Sync Now"
Last sync: relative timestamp ("5 minutes ago") below button
On success: green checkmark animation, drift recalculates to ~0s

Timezone & DST Section (card)
Timezone picker: dropdown showing UTC offsets in 15-min increments
Current: "UTC+02:00 (Israel Standard Time)"
DST toggle switch On/Off, offset display in minutes
"Auto-Detect" button: reads phone timezone, writes both to device

States
Synced: drift <1s, green badge, "Sync Now" secondary priority
Drifted: drift visible, amber/red badge, "Sync Now" prominent
Syncing: button shows spinner, "Synchronizing..."
Disconnected: standard disconnected overlay
