// standard

What is BPMN?

Business Process Model and Notation — an international standard (ISO 19510) for graphically representing business processes in a workflow.

✎

Visual Language

A shared diagram notation that both business and technical teams can understand

⚙

Executable

BPMN diagrams can be directly executed by process engines like Camunda

⌘

Universal Standard

Used worldwide across industries — healthcare, finance, government, and more

// bpmn-elements.gateways

BPMN Gateways

Gateways control the flow of a process — they split, merge, and route paths based on conditions or events.

Exclusive Gateway Diamond shape
  • Exclusive (XOR) — One path based on condition
  • Parallel (AND) — All paths simultaneously
  • Inclusive (OR) — One or more paths
  • Event-Based — Wait for events to decide
