A first project should be significant enough to prove value, yet focused enough that you can learn from it within weeks. Look for a process with repetitive volume, accessible information, a recognized pain point, and a role owner willing to take responsibility for the outcome.

Prefer a process where work can be handed back to a person without hurting the customer. A clear fallback path lets the team experiment, measure, and improve without demanding perfection on day one.
