“A human will review it” sounds reassuring, but it leaves the important questions unanswered. Which actions need review? What context does the reviewer receive? How quickly must they respond, and what happens while the work is waiting?

A useful review step is proportional to risk. Low-impact, reversible actions may be sampled after completion. High-impact actions may require approval before execution. Ambiguous cases need a clear escalation path and enough context for a person to make a better decision than the system.

Treating human-in-the-loop review as part of the product creates a faster and safer process. Treating it as a checkbox usually creates a queue.
